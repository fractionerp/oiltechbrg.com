--- layout: failure-guide title: "Bearing Black Scab and Wire Wool Damage: Causes and Response" description: "Identify black scab and wire wool damage in whitemetal bearings, trace hard-particle contamination and protect the bearing and shaft from recurrence." keywords: "bearing black scab, wire wool bearing damage, whitemetal bearing contamination, shaft scoring, babbitt bearing debris" permalink: /technical-library/bearing-failure/black-scab-wire-wool/ last_modified_at: 2026-07-31 failure_name: Black scab and wire wool damage heading: Bearing Black Scab & Wire Wool Damage hero_text: Trace embedded hard particles that can turn a soft bearing lining into a cutting surface against the shaft. image: /assets/img/failures/black-scab.webp image_alt: Black scab and wire wool damage on a whitemetal bearing intro: >- A hard particle can embed in the compliant whitemetal while remaining proud of the surface. It then acts as a cutting tool against the shaft or journal, producing a narrow groove and sometimes long, wire-like metallic swarf. signs: - Fine wire-like metallic debris in or around the bearing - A local dark, hard inclusion embedded in the lining - A narrow circumferential groove on the bearing and mating shaft - Damage concentrated at one persistent cutting point causes: - Grinding or maintenance debris entering an open bearing - Hard particles left in oil passages after overhaul - Debris from chromium-rich steels or repaired shaft coatings - Inadequate cleaning, flushing or exclusion controls during assembly - Filter bypass or contamination introduced downstream of filtration inspection: - Preserve and identify recovered debris where practical - Inspect the corresponding shaft track and any repaired coating - Review nearby grinding, blasting and coating work during overhaul - Examine galleries, housings, filters and oil samples for similar particles - Check assembly cleanliness and component protection records actions: - Remove the embedded particle and all secondary debris - Clean and flush the complete affected lubrication circuit - Repair and verify the shaft surface where it has been machined - Improve exclusion, covering and cleanliness controls during maintenance - Review filtration location and bypass paths for the operating system repair: >- The bearing and shaft should be assessed as a pair. A shallow local mark may be recoverable after the source is removed, but deeper scoring or embedded contamination can require re-lining and shaft restoration followed by dimensional and surface-finish verification. diagnosis_note: >- Not every strand of debris proves classic wire-wool damage. Material identification and matching the bearing mark to the shaft track help distinguish a cutting particle from general wear debris or damage created during dismantling. ---
