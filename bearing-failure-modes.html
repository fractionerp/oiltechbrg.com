Damage Is Evidence, Not the Complete Diagnosis

Whitemetal bearings are often the first components to record a lubrication, alignment, loading or electrical problem. The visible pattern can narrow the investigation, but several mechanisms may act together. Reliable diagnosis combines bearing inspection with operating history, oil-system data, vibration, shaft condition and machine geometry.

Investigation Method

Confirm the Root Cause

1. Preserve the Evidence

Record bearing position, orientation, damage distribution and mating-surface condition before cleaning or repair. Retain oil, filter and debris samples where useful.

2. Review Machine Data

Correlate the pattern with load, speed, temperature, pressure, flow, vibration, alarms, trips and recent maintenance or process changes.

3. Test the Mechanism

Inspect clearances, alignment, shaft geometry, lubrication paths, material and bond condition to confirm or eliminate each credible failure mechanism.
