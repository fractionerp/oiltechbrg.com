--- layout: failure-guide title: "Whitemetal Bearing Fatigue Failure: Signs, Causes and Response" description: "Identify fatigue cracking and lining loss in whitemetal or Babbitt bearings, investigate contributing loads and temperatures, and plan corrective action." keywords: "whitemetal bearing fatigue, babbitt bearing fatigue failure, white metal bearing cracking, bearing lining fatigue" permalink: /technical-library/bearing-failure/fatigue/ last_modified_at: 2026-07-31 failure_name: Bearing lining fatigue heading: Whitemetal Bearing Fatigue Failure hero_text: Recognise fatigue cracking, investigate the cyclic loading behind it and prevent repeat loss of bearing lining. image: /assets/img/failures/fatigue.webp image_alt: Fatigue cracking and material loss in a whitemetal bearing lining intro: >- Fatigue damage develops when repeated or fluctuating stress initiates cracks in the bearing alloy. Cracks can propagate through the lining and along its interface until sections break away, reducing the effective bearing area and contaminating the oil system. signs: - A connected network of fine or branching cracks - Pitting, flaking or pieces of lining missing from the loaded zone - Cracks that turn and travel near the lining-to-backing interface - Progressively rough damage rather than one smooth wiped area causes: - High cyclic or dynamic loading at operating temperature - Rotor unbalance, vibration or repeated load transients - Excessive temperature reducing the fatigue strength of the alloy - Overload, poor load distribution or unsuitable lining geometry - Shaft deflection, misalignment or contact concentrated at an edge inspection: - Trend bearing metal and oil temperatures before the event - Review vibration, balance history, trips and load transients - Map the crack pattern against the bearing loaded zone - Check shaft geometry, alignment, clearances and contact distribution - Verify alloy, lining thickness and bond condition actions: - Correct abnormal vibration, unbalance and temperature - Review bearing load, geometry, clearance and oil-film performance - Improve load distribution or alignment where damage is localised - Consider a higher-fatigue-strength alloy when duty demands it - Inspect and clean the oil system before returning the machine to service repair: >- Local blending is rarely appropriate once fatigue cracks are established because cracking can extend beyond the visible area. If the backing remains sound and dimensionally acceptable, stripping and re-lining may recover the bearing; otherwise manufacture or re-engineering should be considered. diagnosis_note: >- Fatigue can occur alongside misalignment, overheating or poor bonding. Photographs help classify the visible pattern, but root cause should be confirmed using operating temperatures, vibration history, shaft condition, material records and inspection of the complete bearing. ---
