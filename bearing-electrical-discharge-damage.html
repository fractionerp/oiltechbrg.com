--- layout: failure-guide title: "Electrical Discharge Damage in Whitemetal Bearings" description: "Identify electrical pitting and spark tracking in whitemetal bearings, investigate stray current and shaft magnetism, and prevent repeat damage." keywords: "electrical discharge bearing damage, bearing electrical pitting, spark tracking bearing, shaft current babbitt bearing, whitemetal bearing pitting" permalink: /technical-library/bearing-failure/electrical-discharge/ last_modified_at: 2026-07-31 failure_name: Electrical discharge bearing damage heading: Electrical Discharge Bearing Damage hero_text: Distinguish pitting and spark tracks caused by current flow, then verify the machine grounding and insulation path. image: /assets/img/failures/electrical-discharge.webp image_alt: Electrical discharge pitting in a whitemetal bearing lining intro: >- When electrical potential discharges through a bearing, small arcs can locally melt the lining and mating surface. Repeated events produce pitting, frosting or tracks that degrade the surface and can generate further debris. signs: - Fine pitting or a frosted, matt surface over the loaded area - Isolated craters with locally melted edges - Branching, dotted or circumferential spark tracks - Corresponding marks on the shaft or thrust collar causes: - Stray shaft current or residual shaft magnetism - Ineffective, damaged or contaminated grounding arrangements - Insulation unintentionally bridged by services or instrumentation - Electrical machine faults or unsuitable earthing design - Welding current passing through the shaft and bearing path inspection: - Examine both bearing and mating surface under magnification - Measure shaft magnetism and investigate shaft-voltage history - Check grounding brushes, straps, insulation and monitoring devices - Look for services or instrumentation that bridge insulated components - Review welding and maintenance practices around the machine actions: - Correct the source and intended path of shaft current - Restore effective grounding or insulation for the machine design - Degauss components where residual magnetism is confirmed - Prevent welding return current from crossing bearings - Inspect and clean the oil system if discharge has released debris repair: >- Electrical pitting cannot be solved by replacing the bearing alone. After the electrical path is corrected, assess crater depth, lining integrity and the shaft or collar surface to determine whether re-lining, machining or component replacement is required. diagnosis_note: >- Fine pitting can resemble erosion, corrosion or debris damage. Surface morphology, matching marks, electrical measurements and the machine configuration should be considered together before electrical discharge is assigned as the cause. ---
