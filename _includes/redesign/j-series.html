J Series equalising thrust bearings

Self-levelling thrust pad assemblies with hardened spherical pivots. Designed to share axial load evenly across the pads.

Discuss your requirements View specifications

Design

Equal load across the pads

Internal segments link the pads and allow the thrust face to level under load. The pads share the load evenly, allowing the bearing to operate at higher overall loads.

J Series bearings are interchangeable with common equalising designs.

Specifications

Applications
Pumps, gearboxes, motors and small steam turbines.
Diameter
5 to 23 inches
Pad backing
Bronze, steel or copper-chrome-zirconium (CCZ).
Lining
BS3332-A (ASTM B23 Grade 2) whitemetal.
Instrumentation
Pre-machined pads for your instrumentation or complete assemblies with instrumentation installed.
